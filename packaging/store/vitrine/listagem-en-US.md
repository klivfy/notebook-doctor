## 3. Full Description (up to 10,000 characters)

```text
Notebook Doctor is a system diagnostics, preventive maintenance, and power-efficiency utility built for Windows 10 and Windows 11 desktops and laptops. Designed to translate low-level operating system metrics into plain, transparent explanations, it shows you exactly what is slowing down your PC, filling up your drive, or draining your battery — and provides safe, reversible actions to fix each issue.

WHY CHOOSE NOTEBOOK DOCTOR?

• Transparent Diagnostics & Health Score (0 to 100): monitor real-time processor (CPU) load, memory (RAM) usage, SSD/drive S.M.A.R.T. health, battery wear, and Windows boot duration, accompanied by clear explanations of your computer's current bottleneck.
• Built-in Simulation Mode & Action Log with Undo: preview any optimization before applying it. Simulation Mode shows the exact registry key, service, or system command that would run without changing anything on your PC. When you apply a change, it is recorded in the Actions tab with one-click Undo support.
• Safe, Selective Cleanup: clear user and Windows temporary files, internet cache, Windows Error Reporting (WER) files, crash dumps, and optional browser or developer caches. Notebook Doctor never touches personal documents, saved passwords, browsing history, or active login cookies, and lets you preview the exact file list before deleting anything.
• Process Control & Quick Rescue Tools: spot applications that are not responding ("Hung"), leaking memory, or hogging CPU in the background. Apply Windows Efficiency Mode (EcoQoS) to heavy background apps, put distracting programs to Sleep temporarily, unlock files held by running processes, or restart File Explorer and the graphics driver without rebooting Windows.
• Startup Manager with Smart 2-Minute Delay: see every program that launches at sign-in, how many seconds each adds to boot time, and its battery impact. Reversibly disable unneeded startup items or delay heavy apps by 2 minutes after login so your desktop is responsive right away.
• Disk Space Analyzer & Duplicate Finder: quickly locate the largest folders and files on your SSD or hard drive, view storage breakdown by file type, and find byte-identical duplicate files (verified by SHA-256 content hash) with a visual review before moving extras to the Recycle Bin.
• Laptop Battery Intelligence & Usage Modes: track live discharge rate in Watts (W), factory capacity health, and the top battery-consuming apps over the past 24 hours and 7 days. Switch on Focus Mode (prioritizes your active app), Lap Mode (disables CPU turbo boost to keep your laptop cool), or Survival Mode (extends battery life when away from an outlet).
• 30-Day Performance History & Slowness Heatmap: follow daily and weekly trends for CPU, RAM, free disk space, and health score, see which days and hours your PC experiences peak load, and export complete reports to HTML, PDF, TXT, or CSV.
• Complete Hardware Summary & Native Windows Tools: inspect detailed specifications for your CPU, RAM modules, motherboard, BIOS/UEFI, GPU, storage drives, Wi-Fi link, and online meeting devices (camera & microphone), plus direct shortcuts to Windows integrity checks (SFC, DISM, CHKDSK, and Windows Security).
• 100% Local & Private by Design: all diagnostics and maintenance run locally on your device. Notebook Doctor requires no account, shows no ads, never collects personal files, and works completely offline.
```
